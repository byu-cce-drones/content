## What changed from Fall 2026

- **Week 1 is one combined Thursday class** (Jan 7), like fall's Thursday Sep 3 intro, so Intro and
  Drone Applications stay separate as in the fall. TRUST is assigned on Jan 7, because the first
  flying lab (Lab A) is the morning of Tue Jan 12; that leaves students five days.
- **Two project work days dropped** (fall Nov 24 and Dec 3). Fall's "Class Recap" moves to the last
  Tuesday.
- **Final project flights fit in one week instead of two.** In the fall, groups A and B flew on
  separate Thursdays. With three lab sections, each section flies its own groups in Week 12, and
  Week 13 becomes the weather backup.
- **Show-and-tell moves into the labs.** Fall used two lecture-and-lab days. In winter each lab
  section presents its own groups in Week 14 (up to 6 groups of 2, about 8 min each).

## Open questions

1. **Labs drop from 110 to 50 minutes.** Fall ran one 110-minute Thursday lab (per
   `planning/ai_context.md`); winter has three 50-minute sections. Every lab page in `docs/labs/` was
   written for 110 minutes and will need trimming or splitting. The Rock Canyon flight is the worst
   case: walking out, flying and walking back in 50 minutes may not work, so it may need a closer
   site or two lab weeks. Smaller sections (12 max, against a full class in fall) help: more
   flying time per student, fewer rotations.
2. **Final project pages still say 12 + 5 minutes.** `docs/final_project/presentation.md` and the
   schedule page need the shorter winter format before the semester.
