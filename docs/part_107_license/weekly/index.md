# Part 107 Every Week

!!! abstract "Key Takeaways"
    - Each week's pre-class reading includes **one short Part 107 topic**: about five minutes of reading and a few practice questions.
    - **Each lecture opens with a Part 107 question** on that topic. It is also how lecture attendance is taken, so do the reading and be on time.
    - By the end of the semester you will have seen every area of the FAA knowledge test several times, which is the best preparation for the [Part 107 practice test](../60_question_exam.md) and the real exam.
    - You can take the real FAA test any time; you do not have to wait for the end of the course.

---

## Why a little every week

The FAA Part 107 knowledge test covers regulations, airspace, operations, weather, and aircraft performance. Cramming it in one week does not work well: the rules blur together and the chart questions need practice. Instead, each week adds one piece, tied where possible to what the class is doing that week. Reading a sectional chart lands in the same week as coordinate systems; reading a weather report lands in the week of the Rock Canyon flight.

The two full Part 107 lectures (Week 3 and Week 10) tie the pieces together.
