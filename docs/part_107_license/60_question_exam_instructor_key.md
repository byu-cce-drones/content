### Question 1
Refer to Figure 10. The solid blue concentric rings surrounding KSLC indicate:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Solid blue rings are Class B airspace; Figure 10 labels it SALT LAKE CITY CLASS B. (Chart legend; PHAK ch. 15.)

---
### Question 2 (revised 2026-10-20)
Refer to Figure 10. The number '120/60' within a Class B shelf indicates:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Class B shelf notation is ceiling over floor in hundreds of feet MSL: 12,000 ft MSL over 6,000 ft MSL. Every SLC shelf reads 120/xx because the Class B ceiling is 12,000 ft MSL. The question used to say '100/40', which is not on this chart and whose 4,000 ft floor would be below the valley floor (about 4,200-4,500 ft MSL). (Chart "Examples of Class B Altitudes" box.)

---
### Question 3
Refer to Figure 10. Provo Airport (KPVU) is surrounded by a dashed blue circle. This indicates:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** A dashed blue line is Class D airspace. (Chart legend.)

---
### Question 4
Refer to Figure 10. West of Salt Lake City is R-6402A. This area is:

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** The R- prefix means a Restricted Area (RESTRICTED R-6402 A on the lower-left of Figure 10). Permission from the using or controlling agency is required. (14 CFR 107.45.)

---
### Question 5 (revised 2026-10-20)
Refer to Figure 10. The area labeled 'SEVIER B & D MOAs' southwest of the lake is:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** MOA means Military Operations Area, drawn with magenta hatching. An MOA is not off-limits under Part 107, but it is a hazard; check activity times in the chart margin. The question used to name 'UTTR', which is not a label on this excerpt (the Utah Test and Training Range is the restricted-area complex, not an MOA).

### Question 6
Given METAR: KSLC 121753Z 33012KT 10SM FEW050 SCT120 18/06 A2992 What is the wind?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** 33012KT is wind from 330 degrees true at 12 knots. (PHAK ch. 13; AC 00-45.)

### Question 7
Given METAR: KSLC 121753Z 33012KT 10SM FEW050 SCT120 18/06 A2992 What is the visibility?

### Question 8
Given METAR: KSLC 121753Z 33012KT 10SM FEW050 SCT120 18/06 A2992 What is the temperature and dewpoint?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** 18/06 is temperature 18 C, dew point 6 C.

### Question 9
Given TAF: TAF KSLC 121720Z 1218/1324 32010KT P6SM SCT050 FM130300 35015KT P6SM SKC What change occurs at 0300Z?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** FM130300 starts a new group at 0300Z on the 13th: wind 350 at 15 knots, visibility better than 6 SM, sky clear.

### Question 10 (revised 2026-10-20)
(Refer to Figure 10) You plan to operate at 350 ft AGL near Sandy, beneath the Class B shelf marked 120/60 and outside any Class D or Class E surface area. What is required?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The shelf floor is 6,000 ft MSL, about 1,500 ft above the ground at Sandy. At 350 ft AGL the aircraft is in uncontrolled airspace below the shelf, so no ATC authorization is needed; the 400 ft AGL limit still applies. The question used to cite a '100/40' shelf with a 4,000 ft MSL floor, which would be underground here. (14 CFR 107.41.)

---
### Question 11
What is the maximum groundspeed allowed for a small UAS under Part 107?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** 87 knots (100 mph) groundspeed. (14 CFR 107.51(a).)

---
### Question 12
According to Part 107, what is the minimum visibility required for flight from the control station?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** 3 statute miles flight visibility from the control station. (14 CFR 107.51(c).)

---
### Question 13 (revised 2026-10-20: key changed from B to C)
(Refer to Figure 10) What is the ceiling of the Class D airspace surrounding Provo Airport (KPVU)?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** The bracketed number inside the Class D circle is the ceiling in hundreds of feet MSL. Provo shows **[70]**, so the ceiling is 7,000 ft MSL (about 2,500 ft above the 4,497 ft field). The old key, 4,535 ft MSL, is only 38 ft above the field and matches nothing on the chart. (Chart legend, "Ceiling of Class D Airspace in hundreds of feet".)

---
### Question 14
Which of the following is a "hazardous attitude" as defined by the FAA?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The five hazardous attitudes are anti-authority, impulsivity, invulnerability, macho and resignation. (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 15
Under Part 107, how many days do you have to report an accident that results in serious injury or property damage over $500?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** No later than 10 calendar days, for serious injury or loss of consciousness, or property damage (other than the aircraft) over $500 repair cost or fair market value. (14 CFR 107.9, retitled "Safety event reporting" in 2022; substance unchanged.)

---
### Question 16
What mnemonic helps a pilot assess their physical and mental fitness to fly?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** IMSAFE: Illness, Medication, Stress, Alcohol, Fatigue, Emotion. (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 17 (revised 2026-10-20)
Under Part 107, when must a small unmanned aircraft be registered with the FAA?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Every aircraft flown under Part 107 must be registered, whatever it weighs. The 0.55 lb (250 g) exemption in 14 CFR 48.15(b) applies only to aircraft flown exclusively under the recreational exception (49 U.S.C. 44809). The question used to ask for a weight threshold, which is wrong for Part 107. (14 CFR 48.15; 107.13.)

---
### Question 18
When must a remote pilot yield the right-of-way to all other aircraft?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** At all times: the small UA must yield to all aircraft, airborne vehicles and launch and reentry vehicles. (14 CFR 107.37(a).)

---
### Question 19
What is the "structure exception" to the 400 ft AGL altitude limit?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Within a 400 ft radius of a structure, the aircraft may fly up to 400 ft above the structure's uppermost limit. (14 CFR 107.51(b).)

---
### Question 20
Which weather product provides current observed conditions at an airport?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** A METAR is an observation; a TAF is a forecast; a SIGMET is an advisory; a NOTAM is not weather.

---
### Question 21
What is "density altitude"?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Pressure altitude corrected for non-standard temperature. High, hot and humid means high density altitude and poorer performance. (PHAK ch. 11.)

---
### Question 22
How often must a remote pilot complete recurrent training to maintain currency?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Within the previous 24 calendar months (initial test or free online recurrent training). The certificate itself does not expire. (14 CFR 107.65.)

---
### Question 23 (label corrected 2026-10-20: R-6404 to R-6404A, as charted)
(Refer to Figure 10) The area marked R-6404A is a:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** R- prefix means a Restricted Area (RESTRICTED R-6404 A, upper left of Figure 10). (14 CFR 107.45.)

---
### Question 24
What are the standard cloud clearance requirements for Part 107?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** 500 ft below and 2,000 ft horizontally from clouds. (14 CFR 107.51(d).)

---
### Question 25
The center of gravity (CG) of an aircraft is:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The point about which the aircraft would balance; it moves with loading. (PHAK ch. 10.)

---
### Question 26
Which of these involves the "PAVE" checklist?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External pressures. (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 27
Under Part 107, night operations are permitted if the drone is equipped with:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Anti-collision lighting visible for at least 3 statute miles with a flash rate sufficient to avoid a collision. The pilot must also have taken the initial test or recurrent training after April 6, 2021. (14 CFR 107.29(a).)

---
### Question 28
Which source is used to obtain authorizations for controlled airspace in near-real-time?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** LAANC (Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability) gives near-real-time authorization at or below the UAS Facility Map altitude; FAADroneZone handles the rest. (14 CFR 107.41; FAA LAANC page.)

---
### Question 29
A "TAF" is typically valid for what period of time?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** TAFs cover 24 hours (30 hours at some major airports) and are issued four times a day. (PHAK ch. 13.)

---
### Question 30
What type of fog forms on clear, calm nights when the ground cools rapidly?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Radiation fog. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 31
What is the primary purpose of a "Visual Observer" (VO)?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The VO helps see and avoid other air traffic and objects and scans the airspace for collision hazards. (14 CFR 107.3, 107.33.)

---
### Question 32 (revised 2026-10-20)
(Refer to Figure 10) The "120/60" label on the Class B shelf south of KSLC (near Sandy) indicates a floor of:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The bottom number is the floor in hundreds of feet MSL: 6,000 ft MSL. The question used to cite an '80/40' shelf, which is not on this chart.

---
### Question 33
What is the effect of high humidity on sUAS performance?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Moist air is less dense than dry air, so lift and thrust fall. (PHAK ch. 11.)

---
### Question 34
Which document must be available for inspection upon request by the FAA?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The remote pilot certificate (with identification) must be in physical possession and presented on request; the registration certificate must be available for the aircraft. (14 CFR 107.7; 91.203.)

---
### Question 35
If a remote pilot changes their permanent mailing address, how long do they have to update it with the FAA?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** After 30 days the certificate may not be exercised until the FAA is notified. (14 CFR 107.77(c).)

---
### Question 36
What is the definition of a "Small UAS" under Part 107?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** An unmanned aircraft weighing less than 55 pounds on takeoff, including everything on board or attached. (14 CFR 107.3.)

---
### Question 37
"Stable air" is typically associated with which of the following?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Smooth air, poor visibility, stratiform clouds and steady precipitation. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 38
What should a pilot do if they experience a "flyaway" or lost link in controlled airspace?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Notify ATC as soon as possible if the aircraft could endanger other traffic; the PIC must avoid creating an undue hazard. (AC 107-2A ch. 5; 14 CFR 107.19.)

---
### Question 39
A "Military Operations Area" (MOA) is depicted on a sectional chart by:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Magenta hatched boundary; restricted, prohibited and warning areas use blue hatching. (Chart legend.)

---
### Question 40
According to ADM principles, if you feel "impulsive" during a flight, the antidote is:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** "Not so fast. Think first." (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 41
What is the "standard" lapse rate in the atmosphere?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** About 2 C (3.5 F) per 1,000 ft. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 42
Under Part 107, can a remote pilot operate from a moving vehicle?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** From a moving land or water vehicle only over a sparsely populated area and not carrying another person's property for hire; never from a moving aircraft. (14 CFR 107.25.)

---
### Question 43
What type of cloud has the most significant vertical development and is associated with thunderstorms?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Cumulonimbus. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 44 (revised 2026-10-20)
(Refer to Figure 10) The blue obstruction symbol labeled "4656 (408)" beside the Stadium in Salt Lake City represents:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The upright number is the top of the obstruction in feet MSL (4,656) and the number in parentheses is its height above the ground (408 ft AGL). The question used to cite "(512)", which appears nowhere on this excerpt. (Chart legend, "Obstructions".)

---
### Question 45
What does the "A" in the IMSAFE checklist stand for?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Alcohol: no flying within 8 hours of drinking or at 0.04 or more blood alcohol. (14 CFR 107.27; 91.17.)

---
### Question 46
A remote pilot must perform a pre-flight inspection of the UAS:

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Before every flight. (14 CFR 107.49.)

---
### Question 47
"Load factor" increases during:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** In a level turn the wings or rotors must support more than the aircraft's weight (1.15 G at 30 degrees, 2 G at 60 degrees). (PHAK ch. 5.)

---
### Question 48
Which of the following is true regarding Remote ID?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Remote ID broadcasts the aircraft's identity, position and control-station (or takeoff) location in real time. It is required for every aircraft that must be registered, so it applies to all Part 107 aircraft, and it does not replace registration. (14 CFR 89.101, 89.105.)

---
### Question 49
What is the floor of Class E airspace in most of the United States (unless otherwise marked)?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** 1,200 ft AGL by default, 700 ft AGL inside the magenta vignette, or the surface where a dashed magenta line is charted. Only the surface area needs ATC authorization. (Chart legend; 14 CFR 107.41.)

---
### Question 50
If the temperature and dew point are within 3 degrees of each other, what is likely to form?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Fog or low clouds. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 51
Who is ultimately responsible for the safe operation of a small UAS?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** The remote pilot in command has final authority and is directly responsible. (14 CFR 107.19.)

---
### Question 52
What is the risk of "spatial disorientation"?

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Losing track of the aircraft's orientation or position, which is why line of sight and a visual observer matter. (PHAK ch. 17.)

---
### Question 53 (revised 2026-10-20: "hatched brown" corrected to "hatched blue")
(Refer to Figure 10) The hatched blue boundary labeled R-6402A indicates:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Blue hatching marks prohibited, restricted and warning areas; R-6402A is restricted. (Chart legend.)

---
### Question 54
"Thermal plumes" (updrafts) are caused by:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Uneven heating of the Earth's surface. (PHAK ch. 12.)

---
### Question 55
A "Waiver" from the FAA allows you to:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** A Certificate of Waiver lets you deviate from the sections listed in 14 CFR 107.205 (for example 107.31 line of sight, 107.39 over people, 107.51 operating limits) if you show it can be done safely. (14 CFR 107.200, 107.205.)

---
### Question 56
Which frequency should a pilot use to announce their position at a non-towered airport?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The CTAF, shown on the chart with a white C in a magenta circle after the frequency. (PHAK ch. 14.)

---
### Question 57
Under Part 107, "Civil Twilight" is defined as:

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** 30 minutes before official sunrise to sunrise, and sunset to 30 minutes after official sunset (outside Alaska). Anti-collision lights visible 3 SM are required. (14 CFR 107.29(b)-(c).)

---
### Question 58
What is the primary risk of flying with a "Macho" attitude?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Taking risks to prove yourself; antidote: "Taking chances is foolish." (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 59
In the "PAVE" checklist, the "E" stands for:

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** External pressures. (PHAK ch. 2.)

---
### Question 60
What should a pilot do if they encounter a manned aircraft while flying their drone?

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Yield the right of way and never pass over, under or ahead of it unless well clear. (14 CFR 107.37.)
