## The four turn-in points

1. **[Project Proposal](proposal.md)** — your project, site, and flight plan, before you fly.
2. **[Flight and Data Processing](flight_and_data_processing.md)** — your flight record and processed
   products, due one week after your group flies.
3. **[Project Presentation](presentation.md)** — slides uploaded Monday Dec 7; present in class on
   your signed-up day.
4. **[Technical Memo](report.md)** — one page of writing plus figures, due Thursday Dec 10.

---

## Planning your time

- **Before your flight:** proposal approved, flight plan loaded, roles decided, checklist printed.
- **Flight week:** fly, then start processing **that same week**. Bentley processing can run for
  hours, and the lab machines are shared.
- **Work days (Nov 24, Dec 3):** finish your products, then start the slides and memo. TAs are in
  EB 321 to help.
- **Need more processing time?** Contact the TAs early. The last week fills up.
