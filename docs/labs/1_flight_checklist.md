# Flight Checklist Lab

## Batteries & Power
Charge all necessary drone flight batteries to 100%
Charge the remote controller (RC) battery fully
Charge your phone or tablet
Pack a portable power bank (optional but recommended for longer missions)
Drone Hardware & Accessories
Inspect all propellers for cracks, chips, or warping
Inspect propellers for security and stability

* Insert microSD card — verify it's formatted with enough free space for your mission
* Pack a landing pad
* Phone/Controller & Software

Update drone firmware (aircraft & RC) to latest version
Update the manufacturer's flight app (e.g. DJI Fly, Autel Sky)
Open flight app and confirm drone connects correctly

1. [ ] Check airspace with B4UFLY, AirMap, or LAANC app for TFRs and restricted zones
2. [ ] Download offline maps for the flying area if needed
3. [ ] Regulatory & Documentation
4. [ ] Carry your FAA Remote Pilot Certificate or confirm recreational rules apply
5. [ ] Confirm FAA registration number is affixed to the drone

Obtain any required LAANC authorization or waivers
Check local rules, park permits, or landowner permissions
Review weather forecast for wind, gusts, precipitation, and cloud ceiling
Personal & Safety Items

1. Sunglasses
2. Sun protection / hat if flying in an open area
3. Water bottle
4. Closed toe comfortable shoes
