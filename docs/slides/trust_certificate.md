# Get Your TRUST Certificate

## Required before you fly in Week 2

---

## What TRUST is

Anyone flying a drone recreationally in the US has to pass **TRUST**, The Recreational UAS Safety
Test — free, online, about 30 minutes, and designed so that everyone passes.

You may not fly **any** drone in this course, including the indoor mini drones, until you hold a
TRUST certificate. The first flying lab is Week 2.

---

## Taking the test

1. Pick any FAA-approved test administrator — they all give the same test.
2. Work through the reading and the questions. About 30 minutes.
3. Download the completion certificate as a PDF.
4. Submit it to the TRUST Certificate assignment in Learning Suite.

---

## Download it when you finish

The FAA does not keep a copy. The certificate is issued to you once — if you lose it, your only
option is to take the test again.

Download the PDF, email it to yourself, and keep a copy on your phone.

---

## Where to submit

Upload the PDF to the **TRUST Certificate** assignment in Learning Suite.

<p class="caption">Deadline: before the Week 2 flying lab. No certificate, no flying.</p>

---

## TRUST is not Part 107

- **TRUST** — the recreational-style flying you do in this class.
- **Part 107** — the separate, harder certificate for flying commercially, earned by passing an FAA
  knowledge test at a testing center. This course prepares you for it later.
