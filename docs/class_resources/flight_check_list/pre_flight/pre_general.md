# General Pre-Flight Checklist

A comprehensive pre-flight checklist is essential for ensuring the safety and success of your flight operations. This checklist covers general items that should be reviewed before every flight.

---

<h1>Flight Checklist</h1>

<h2>Before Leaving the House/Building</h2>

<h3>Batteries &amp; Power</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Charge all necessary <strong>drone flight batteries</strong> to 100%</li>
  <li>Charge the <strong>remote controller (RC) battery</strong> fully</li>
  <li>Charge your <strong>phone or tablet</strong></li>
  <li>Pack a <strong>portable power bank</strong> (optional but recommended for longer missions)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Drone Hardware &amp; Accessories</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Inspect all <strong>propellers</strong> for cracks, chips, or warping</li>
  <li>Inspect propellers for <strong>security and stability</strong></li>
  <li>Insert <strong>microSD card</strong> — verify it's formatted with enough free space for your mission</li>
  <li>Pack a <strong>landing pad</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3>Phone/Controller &amp; Software</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Update <strong>drone software</strong> (aircraft &amp; RC) to latest version</li>
  <li>Update the manufacturer's <strong>flight app</strong> (e.g. DJI Fly, Autel Sky)</li>
  <li>Open flight app and confirm <strong>drone connects</strong> correctly</li>
  <li>Check airspace with <strong>B4UFLY, AirMap, or LAANC</strong> app for TFRs and restricted zones</li>
  <li>Download <strong>offline maps</strong> for the flying area if needed</li>
</ul>

<h3>Regulatory &amp; Documentation</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Carry your <strong>FAA Remote Pilot Certificate</strong> or confirm recreational rules apply</li>
  <li>Confirm <strong>FAA registration number</strong> is affixed to the drone</li>
  <li>Obtain any required <strong>LAANC authorization or waivers</strong></li>
  <li>Check local rules, park permits, or <strong>landowner permissions</strong></li>
  <li>Review <strong>weather forecast</strong> for wind, gusts, precipitation, and cloud ceiling</li>
</ul>

<h3>Personal &amp; Safety Items</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Sunglasses</li>
  <li>Sun protection / hat if flying in an open area</li>
  <li>Water bottle</li>
  <li>Closed toe comfortable shoes</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>On Arrival to Mission Site</h2>

<h3>Weather &amp; Environment</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Check actual <strong>wind speed and direction</strong> on site</li>
  <li>Note <strong>sun position</strong> relative to your flight path (for eye protection)</li>
  <li>Assess <strong>temperature</strong> — cold significantly shortens battery life</li>
  <li>Confirm no <strong>rain or fog</strong> — most drones are not waterproof</li>
</ul>

<h3>Airspace &amp; Site Hazards</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Identify all tall obstacles: <strong>buildings, towers, cranes, bridges</strong></li>
  <li>Look for <strong>power lines, utility poles, and guy wires</strong></li>
  <li>Identify <strong>trees</strong> — note height and canopy spread in your flight path</li>
  <li>Check for <strong>antenna arrays or cell towers</strong> nearby that could interfere with GPS</li>
  <li>Verify you will stay below the <strong>400 ft AGL limit</strong> unless authorized otherwise</li>
  <li>Confirm no active <strong>TFRs</strong> or pop-up restricted airspace in your app</li>
  <li>Look and listen for <strong>manned aircraft, helicopters, and crop dusters</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3>People &amp; Surroundings</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Identify <strong>bystanders</strong> and keep them clear of the takeoff area</li>
  <li>Locate nearby <strong>roads, parking lots, and pedestrian paths</strong></li>
  <li>Note any <strong>animals</strong> (horses, dogs, livestock) that could be startled</li>
  <li>Identify a safe <strong>emergency landing zone</strong> away from people and vehicles</li>
</ul>

<h3>Launch Site Setup</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Choose a takeoff point with <strong>clear sightlines</strong> in all directions</li>
  <li>Place <strong>landing pad</strong> on a flat, clear, debris-free surface
    <ul>
      <li>In the absence of a landing pad, a clean, debris-free, flat surface will suffice</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Orient yourself so <strong>Return-to-Home</strong> brings the drone back safely</li>
</ul>

<h3>Aircraft &amp; Controller Final Check</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Insert a charged battery and confirm it <strong>clicks/locks</strong> in place</li>
  <li>Power on the <strong>RC first</strong>, then power on the drone</li>
  <li>Open flight app and confirm <strong>live video feed</strong> is active</li>
  <li>Wait for <strong>GPS lock</strong> — confirm satellite count is 8 or higher</li>
  <li>Check <strong>compass and IMU calibration</strong> status — recalibrate if prompted</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Return-to-Home altitude</strong> above the tallest obstacle in the flight path</li>
  <li>Check <strong>RC signal strength</strong> and confirm controls are responding</li>
  <li>Arm motors briefly to confirm <strong>all props spin freely and evenly</strong></li>
  <li>Do a <strong>low 6-foot hover</strong> for 10–15 seconds to check stability before flying out</li>
  <li>Enjoy your mission</li>
</ul>
