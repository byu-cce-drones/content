# General Post-Flight Checklist

The flight is not finished when the drone lands. Running the same checks after every flight
protects the aircraft, secures the data you just collected, and leaves the equipment ready for the
next crew. Work through this list before you leave the site.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Aircraft powered off first, then the controller
- [ ] Propellers inspected for chips, cracks, and bends
- [ ] Airframe, arms, and landing feet checked for damage
- [ ] Gimbal cover replaced and control sticks stowed
- [ ] Batteries removed and allowed to cool before charging
- [ ] Battery levels and any warnings noted
- [ ] Images and flight logs copied off the memory card
- [ ] Data reviewed for coverage and quality **before leaving the site**
- [ ] Flight log completed: time, location, conditions, and anything unusual
- [ ] Any incident, damage, or erratic behavior reported to the instructor
- [ ] All equipment packed and accounted for

!!! tip "Check the data before you pack up"
    Reviewing your images on site costs a few minutes. Discovering a gap in coverage back at the
    lab costs a return trip.

---

## Drone-Specific Checks

Aircraft differ in how batteries are released, how the gimbal is protected, and how logs are
retrieved. Check the manual for the drone you flew, and see
[Flight Basics](../../../gen_reading/flight_basics.md) for what each part does.

Drone-specific checklists will be linked here as they are added.
