# Class Schedule — Winter 2027

!!! abstract "Key Takeaways"
    - **This schedule is for Winter 2027** (classes Wed Jan 6 – Wed Apr 14, 2027).
    - **Lecture:** Tuesdays 8:00–8:50 in **CTB 250**. Everyone attends.
    - **Lab:** you attend **one** lab time each week, all in **EB B122** (one exception: the Week 9
      lab is in **CB 308**). This semester, lab times are **assigned at random**; see
      [Your lab time](#when-and-where-we-meet) to switch.
    - **First class is Thursday, Jan 7, 8:00–9:50 in CTB 250**, with everyone together.
    - Your **TRUST certificate is due Sat Jan 9 at 11:59 PM**. You cannot fly without it.
    - Late work loses 10% per week, up to 50%. Nothing is accepted after **Wed Apr 14, 11:59 PM**.

## When and where we meet

| Meeting | Day | Time | Room |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lecture (everyone) | Tuesday | 8:00–8:50 | CTB 250 |
| Lab A | Tuesday | 9:00–9:50 | EB B122 |
| Lab B | Thursday | 8:00–8:50 | EB B122 |
| Lab C | Thursday | 9:00–9:50 | EB B122 |

All three lab times do the same lab in the same week. Go to your assigned lab time every week.

!!! note "Your lab time this semester"
    **For Winter 2027, lab times are assigned at random.** If you need a different lab time for
    the whole semester, find a student in that lab time who will trade with you. Then **both of you
    talk to the professor or the head TA** to make the switch.

    You cannot switch lab times week to week. If you need to attend another lab time for **one
    week**, tell the professor or the head TA **by the end of your own lab one week before**. They
    will decide whether you can just attend or need to find someone to trade with for that week.
    See [Lab Times](syllabus.md#lab-times) in the syllabus.

!!! note "Weeks with no lab"
    When there is no class on the Tuesday **or** the Thursday of a week, **all three lab times
    are cancelled that week**, so every lab time stays on the same lab. That happens in Weeks 1, 7
    and 15.

### Late work

- Late work loses **10% of the assignment's full points for each week it is late**: 1 to 7 days
  late is 10% off, 8 to 14 days is 20% off, and so on. The penalty stops at **50%**.
- The penalty comes off the points possible, not your score. On a 20-point assignment, one week
  late is 2 points off whatever you earn.
- **Pre-class reading quizzes cannot be turned in late.**
- **Nothing is accepted after Wed Apr 14, 11:59 PM**, the last day of classes. Work due in Week 1
  can still be turned in that last week, for 50% off.
- A late TRUST certificate still costs points, and you cannot fly until it is in.

### Attendance

- Attendance is taken **at the start** of every lecture and lab. Arrive late and you miss it.
- In the two project flight weeks, only the groups flying that week have lab, and attendance is
  taken at the start of their flight time. If neither partner shows up, the group reschedules its
  flight with a TA.
