# The TRUST Certificate

!!! abstract "Key Takeaways"
    - **You may not fly any drone in this course, including the indoor mini drones, until you hold a
      TRUST certificate.** The first flying lab is in Week 2, so do this in Week 1.
    - TRUST is free, online, takes about 30 minutes, and is designed so that everyone passes.
    - The FAA does not keep a copy. Download the certificate when you finish and keep it.
    - Submit it to the TRUST Certificate assignment in Learning Suite. It counts toward your grade.
    - TRUST is not Part 107. Part 107 is the separate certificate for flying commercially, and the
      course prepares you for it later.

---

## What TRUST is

Anyone flying a drone recreationally in the United States has to pass **TRUST**, The Recreational
UAS Safety Test. UAS stands for unmanned aircraft system, the FAA's term for a drone together with
its controller. You need your certificate before the first lab, and you will not be allowed to fly
without it.

It is not an exam in any meaningful sense. It is a short set of questions with a bit of reading
attached, and it is designed so that everyone passes.

| | |
|---|---|
| Cost | Free |
| Where | Online, through any FAA-approved administrator |
| How long | About 30 minutes |
| Can you fail it? | No. Miss a question and it shows you the answer, then asks again |
| Does it expire? | No |
| Do you carry it? | Yes, whenever you fly |

---

## What it covers

The basics you would want any pilot near you to know: where you may and may not fly, staying below
400 feet (120 m), keeping the aircraft in sight, giving way to crewed aircraft, and when a drone has
to be registered. All of it comes back in [Flight Basics](flight_basics.md) and again, in much more
depth, in the Part 107 material.

---

## Taking the test

1. Open the FAA's list of approved test administrators and pick any one of them. They all give the
   same test.

    [Take the TRUST test](https://www.faa.gov/uas/recreational_flyers/knowledge_test_updates){target="_blank"}

2. Work through the reading and the questions. Allow about 30 minutes.
3. When you finish, **download the completion certificate as a PDF.**

!!! warning "Save your certificate when you finish"
    The FAA does not keep a copy. The certificate is issued to you once, and if you lose it your
    only option is to take the test again. Download the PDF, email it to yourself, and keep a copy
    on your phone. You are expected to have it with you whenever you fly.

---

## Submitting it

Upload the PDF to the **TRUST Certificate** assignment in Learning Suite. The syllabus counts it
under Exams, and you must have it on file before you take part in any flight lab. See Learning Suite
for the due date.

---

## TRUST is not Part 107

TRUST covers the recreational-style flying you do in this class. **Part 107** is the separate, harder
certificate you need to fly for work, earned by passing an FAA knowledge test at a testing center.
[FAA Part 107 Overview](../part_107_license/faa_exam_planning_and_overview.md) introduces the rules
in [Week 3](../weeks/week_03.md), and [Week 9](../weeks/week_09.md) is exam preparation.
